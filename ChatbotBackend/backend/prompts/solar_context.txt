# SOLAR ENERGY KNOWLEDGE BASE
# For accurate, context-aware responses

## KEY CONCEPTS & TERMINOLOGY
**Production Metrics:**
- kW (kilowatt): Instantaneous power
- kWh (kilowatt-hour): Energy over time
- Efficiency: AC/DC ratio, typically 85-95%
- Capacity Factor: Actual output / maximum possible output
- Peak Sun Hours: Hours of equivalent full-power sun

**System Components:**
- PV Panels: Mono vs Polycrystalline, efficiency ratings
- Inverters: String vs Micro vs Hybrid
- Batteries: Lithium-ion, lead-acid, flow batteries
- Monitoring: Smart meters, CT clamps, production meters
- Racking: Fixed-tilt vs tracking systems

**Common Calculations:**
- Self-Consumption Rate = (Solar used immediately) / (Total solar production)
- Export Rate = (Solar exported to grid) / (Total solar production)
- System ROI = (Annual savings) / (System cost)
- Carbon Savings = (Solar kWh) × (Grid emission factor)

**Typical Performance Ranges:**
- Residential system: 3-10 kW
- Daily production per kW: 3-5 kWh (varies by location)
- Panel efficiency: 15-22% for commercial panels
- Inverter efficiency: 95-98%
- Battery round-trip efficiency: 85-95%

**Optimization Strategies:**
- Load Shifting: Move consumption to solar production hours
- Peak Shaving: Reduce grid draw during expensive rate periods
- Smart Charging: Optimize EV/Battery charging schedules
- Weather Adaptation: Adjust expectations based on forecast
